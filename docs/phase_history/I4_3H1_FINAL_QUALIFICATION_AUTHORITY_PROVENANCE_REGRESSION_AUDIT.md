# I4.3H1 — Final Qualification, Authority/Provenance & Regression Audit

## Scope

I4.3H1 is the final **qualification/audit** boundary for the I4.3 Production Dense-Provider Architecture. It does not formally close I4.3; that responsibility remains I4.3H2.

H1 is execution-light and evidence-driven. It consumes the frozen formal I4.3G5B closure, independently re-hashes its five immediate G1/G2/G3/G4/G5A predecessor bindings, re-audits the current in-repository provider/adaptive-planning contract surfaces, and binds a consolidated automated-regression receipt covering H1 plus the complete predecessor I4.3 unit/integration/stress suite.

No provider backend, PyCOLMAP/SfM, matched-view renderer or manual-review workflow is rerun by H1.

## Provider architecture audit

H1 re-validates the canonical I4.3A vocabulary and current implementation surfaces:

- five family contracts: MVS, multi-view AI depth, temporal depth, single-image depth, and reserved Hybrid;
- four executable/authorized families; Hybrid remains contract-only and unauthorized;
- ten concrete provider implementations: COLMAP MVS, DA3, RollingDepth, Video Depth Anything, Metric3D v2, UniDepthV2, MoGe-2, Depth Pro, Depth Anything V2 and Marigold;
- every implementation keeps its frozen family, native-output, frame-binding and integration-stage contract;
- every production adapter module/class, descriptor and binding validator remains importable without starting a backend runtime;
- all ten adaptive view-selection requirement records remain valid;
- the adaptive relative-cost values are planning coefficients, not measured runtime/VRAM guarantees;
- `permanent_global_view_cap=None` remains frozen.

All ten implementations are recorded as integrated and covered by closed scenario-family qualification evidence. This is **not** a global promotion and does not make any provider universally preferred.

## Operating envelopes and failure modes

H1 carries forward the formally closed G operating envelope rather than creating a new score. Production-native evidence remains primary and matched-view/manual evidence remains secondary.

The five frozen terminal quality events remain explicit:

- RollingDepth degraded on temporal static sweep;
- RollingDepth rejected on temporal motion;
- Video Depth Anything degraded on temporal motion;
- Depth Anything V2 rejected on single-image low texture;
- Marigold rejected on single-image low texture.

Absence of a terminal event for another provider/scenario is not a universal robustness claim. Direct cross-family ranking remains invalid.

## Provenance audit

H1 SHA256-binds the G5B closure and independently re-hashes the five immediate predecessor artifacts recorded by G5B. It preserves the already-closed family provenance conclusions and integration-stage contracts. H1 deliberately does **not** claim to recursively re-hash every raw/native artifact in the historical evidence tree; those remain preserved through their existing family-specific frozen evidence/closure contracts.

## Regression gate

The H1 regression receipt is produced only after:

- focused H1 unit/integration/stress tests pass;
- all predecessor I4.3 unit tests pass;
- all predecessor I4.3 integration tests pass;
- all predecessor I4.3 stress tests pass;
- H1 import and runner-help probes pass.

The predecessor frozen baseline is 91 test files / 668 tests: 444 unit, 86 integration and 138 stress tests.

## Authority locks

H1 preserves:

- PyCOLMAP as sole authoritative offline camera/scene-frame owner;
- no provider selected, promoted or ranked;
- no universal winner/global scalar score;
- Auto unimplemented;
- Hybrid unauthorized;
- no physical metric-scale claim;
- no measurement or certified-metrology authority;
- no surface mesh;
- no permanent global view cap.

## H1 decision boundary

A passing H1 audit records:

- `i4_3h1_qualification_complete=true`;
- `i4_3h2_authorized=true`;
- `i4_3_closed=false`;
- `i4_4_authorized=false`.

Only I4.3H2 may formally close I4.3 and authorize I4.4.
